Privacy Policy for Fiverr Freelancers Finder

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Fiverr Freelancers Finder ("the Extension") is a Chrome extension that suggests relevant Fiverr services based on the website you are browsing. This policy explains what the Extension accesses, what it stores, and what it does not do.

Summary

The Extension does not collect personal information, does not send your browsing data to us or to any server we operate, and does not use analytics or tracking. Everything it reads from a page is processed locally in your browser.

What the Extension accesses

  • Website address (URL and domain). To suggest relevant services, the Extension reads the address of the page you are viewing. This happens locally in your browser. The address is not sent to us.
  • Basic page appearance. On supported websites, the Extension may read a page's logo and main colors to style a small suggestion prompt shown on that page. This is processed locally and not stored or transmitted.
  • Pages on fiverr.com. On fiverr.com the Extension adds affiliate tracking to Fiverr links and navigation (see "Affiliate disclosure").

The Extension runs a script on web pages in order to detect supported websites and show its suggestion prompt. It does not read form fields, passwords, payment details, cookies, or the content of your messages and accounts.

What the Extension stores

Your filter preferences, custom search settings, and custom keyword mappings are saved using Chrome's storage (chrome.storage). Settings may sync across your own devices if you have Chrome sync enabled. This data stays within your browser and Google account's Chrome sync. We cannot see it. Uninstalling the Extension removes locally stored data.

What we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, or other personal information
  • Your browsing history
  • Passwords, payment information, or other sensitive data
  • Analytics, usage statistics, or advertising identifiers

Network requests

The Extension only loads Fiverr search results (fiverr.com) when you ask it to search. It does not make requests to any server operated by us and does not send your data to third parties.

Affiliate disclosure

The Extension uses Fiverr affiliate links. When you open or navigate Fiverr pages through the Extension, links may carry an affiliate identifier, and we may earn a commission from qualifying purchases at no extra cost to you. Once you are on fiverr.com, Fiverr's own privacy policy and terms apply, and Fiverr may collect data according to its own practices.

Permissions

  • activeTab: to read the address of the tab you are using in order to suggest relevant services.
  • storage: to save your preferences.
  • Host access to fiverr.com: to display search results and apply affiliate tracking on Fiverr.
  • Content script on all websites: to detect supported websites and show a suggestion prompt. No page content is transmitted anywhere.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.

Children

The Extension is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do.

Contact

Questions or requests: please open an issue at github.com/omdsupplier-cpu/fiverr-freelancers-finder/issues.
